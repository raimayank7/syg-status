Friendship status

Lines for the people who know your old nicknames. Tag them, or do not. They will know.

English

  • We do not have photos together because we were busy laughing.

  • My friends never knock. They ask what is for lunch.

  • Same jokes for ten years. Still funny.

  • My friends are the reason my phone storage is full.

  • We met by chance and stayed out of stubbornness.

  • A friend knows your stories. A best friend was in them.

  • Low maintenance friendship: no calls for months, then four hours straight.

  • Ten minutes with the right friend fixes most Mondays.

  • I do not have many friends. I have the right ones.

  • Distance is nothing when the group chat is alive.

  • We were not always wise, but we were always together.

  • हम वो दोस्त हैं जो साथ में डाँट खाते थे।

  • शरारती हैं, पर अपने हैं।

  • दोस्त कम हैं, पर दमदार हैं।

  • स्कूल की बेंच से ज़िंदगी की रेस तक, साथ वही है।

  • जहाँ यार, वहीं त्योहार।
