Fitness and morning walk status

For the post workout photo and the proud "walked today" update. These lines encourage. They do not preach, and they make no health claims.

English

  • Showed up. That was the hard part.

  • Day one, again. Still counts.

  • Morning walk done. The rest of the day is a bonus.

  • Sweat now. Sit proudly later.

  • Slow run is still a run.

  • My bed and my shoes had an argument. Shoes won today.

  • Sore today. Smug tomorrow.

  • Yoga mat: the only place I am this calm.

  • My excuses came along. They sat on the bench.

  • Ten minutes is better than zero minutes.

  • Not here for a six pack. Here to climb stairs without a speech.

  • Walked far enough to deserve the paratha.

हिन्दी

  • आज उठ गया, यही सबसे बड़ी कसरत थी।

  • सुबह की सैर, दिन भर की ताज़गी।

  • बहाने कम, कदम ज़्यादा।

  • धीरे चलो, पर रोज़ चलो।

  • योग से दिन की शुरुआत, मन शांत।

  • आज जिम गया था। यह सूचना इसलिए, क्योंकि रोज़ नहीं जाता।

  • थोड़ा थोड़ा रोज़, यही असली कसरत है।

  • जूते पहन लिए, आधा काम हो गया।
