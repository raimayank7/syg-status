    How to use this site

    1. Choose a mood or a moment above, or type a word in the search box (for example: chai, exam, sunset).
    2. Tap Copy and paste the line into your WhatsApp status, your About, or under a photo. WhatsApp opens the app with the line ready to send to a chat. Save keeps it on this device.
    3. Change one word to make it yours. Our short guide, How to write a good status, shows how, and has a safety checklist.

    What kind of lines are these?

    Short, clean and original. Every line was written by us for this site. You will not find film dialogues, song lyrics or the same lines that have gone around for years. The attitude lines are confident without insulting anyone, the sad lines are honest without bitterness, and everything is safe to post where your family can see it.

    Hindi lines are in Devanagari and Punjabi lines in Gurmukhi, written in those languages and not translated from the English ones.

    Questions people ask

    Can I use these lines on Instagram and Facebook too?

    Yes. They work as a WhatsApp status, a WhatsApp About line, an Instagram caption or bio, or a Facebook post. Copy and paste anywhere.

    Are the lines original?

    Yes. Every line is our own writing. We do not use film dialogues, song lyrics or lines copied from other sites.

    Is there status in Hindi and Punjabi?

    Yes. Most pages have lines in हिन्दी and many in ਪੰਜਾਬੀ. Use the language filter on a page, or open the Hindi and Punjabi pages.

    Does the WhatsApp button post my status?

    No. It opens WhatsApp with the line typed, ready to send to a chat. To post it as a status, tap Copy, open WhatsApp, go to Updates and paste it in a new text status.

    Are the lines safe for family groups?

    Yes. There is no bad language, nothing adult and nothing that insults a person or a group.
